Traditional Kingdoms

Explore Ghana's traditional kingdoms, historic states and diverse cultural heritage through carefully curated photographs, documents and trusted resources. Discover the histories, festivals, customs and traditions that continue to shape communities across the country.

About this Collection

Ghana's traditional kingdoms and states have rich histories shaped by migration, trade, leadership, diplomacy and cultural traditions. From the Asante Kingdom and Dagbon to the Ga States and Anlo, these societies developed distinct systems of governance, social organisation and cultural expression that have influenced Ghana's historical development.

This collection brings together historical documents, photographs, scholarly publications and histories that offer trusted resources about these kingdoms, institutions and heritage traditions. It provides visitors an opportunity to discover the histories and understand the continuing importance of traditional authority and cultural identity in Ghana today.

The Asante Kingdom

Discover the significance of the Golden Stool, the kingdom's political institutions, its encounters with British colonial rule and the traditions that continue to shape Asante identity today.
