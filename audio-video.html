Ghana: A New Nation (1957)

Date: 7 March 1957
Category: Historical Newsreel
Creator: Universal Newsreels
Source: Wikimedia Commons
Format: Archival Video

Access: Free online

This historical newsreel documents celebrations surrounding Ghana's independence in March 1957. It features Kwame Nkrumah and international representatives attending events marking the birth of the new nation.

Kwame Nkrumah's Independence Speech (1957)

Date: 6 March 1957
Category: Historical Speech
Speaker: Dr. Kwame Nkrumah
Location: Old Accra Polo Grounds, Ghana
Format: Historical Video

Access: Free online

This historical footage captures Dr. Kwame Nkrumah delivering his independence speech at the Old Accra Polo Grounds on 6 March 1957. The speech marked Ghana's independence from British colonial rule and expressed his vision for Ghana and the liberation of Africa.

Kwame Nkrumah's Speech at the All-African Peoples' Conference (1958)

Date: December 1958
Category: Historical Speech
Speaker: Dr. Kwame Nkrumah
Location: Accra, Ghana
Format: Historical Video

Access: Free online

This recording features Dr. Kwame Nkrumah addressing the All-African Peoples' Conference held in Accra in December 1958. The conference brought together representatives of African nationalist and liberation movements to discuss colonialism, independence, and African unity.
