Cocoa, Gold & the Colonial Economy

Explore how cocoa farming, gold mining and trade shaped the economy of the colonial Gold Coast. Discover the contributions of African farmers, miners and traders, and how expanding industries, colonial policies and international markets transformed livelihoods and communities before Ghana's independence in 1957.

About this Collection

During the colonial period, cocoa farming, gold mining and commercial trade played important roles in transforming the Gold Coast's economy. African farmers, miners, traders and workers contributed to expanding industries, while colonial policies, transport networks and international demand influenced how goods were produced and exchanged.

This collection brings together historical photographs, documents, publications and other resources exploring economic activities, livelihoods and the experiences of people who shaped the Gold Coast's economy.
