Gold Coast Survey: General Plan of Accra (Provisional)

This historical survey map illustrates Accra's streets, settlements, public buildings, and coastal areas during the colonial Gold Coast period. It offers valuable insight into the city's urban layout and development.

Gold Coast and Ivory Coast: Anglo-French Boundary

Historical period: Early twentieth century

Source: Library of Congress, Geography and Map Division

This historical map illustrates the boundary between the Gold Coast and Ivory Coast during the colonial period. It shows settlements, geographical features, and the territorial boundary established through Anglo-French agreements, providing insight into how colonial borders were mapped in West Africa.

Map of the Gold Coast Railway: Feeder Roads and Agricultural and Mineral Areas

Category: Historical Railway Map

Source: The National Archives (UK)

Archival reference: CO 1069/42/89

This historical map illustrates the railway network, feeder roads, and major agricultural and mineral-producing areas of the Gold Coast. It highlights connections between towns, transport routes, and areas associated with cocoa cultivation, gold mining, and other economic activities during the colonial period.
