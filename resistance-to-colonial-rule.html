Resistance to Colonial Rule

Explore how people in the Gold Coast challenged British colonial rule through armed resistance, political movements, petitions, protests and organised campaigns. Discover the individuals, communities and events that shaped the struggle for rights, representation and self-government.

About this Collection

Resistance to British colonial rule in the Gold Coast took many forms. Communities and traditional leaders challenged colonial expansion and interference in local affairs, while educated Africans, merchants and political organisations opposed policies affecting land ownership, political representation and economic opportunities.

This collection brings together historical documents, photographs, publications and other resources that explore the people, events and movements involved in challenging colonial authority before Ghana's independence in 1957.
