Everyday Life in the Gold Coast

Discover what everyday life was like in the colonial Gold Coast through historical photographs, documents, and stories. Explore how people lived, worked, traded, learned, and participated in their communities.

From bustling markets and traditional occupations to education, clothing, and family life, uncover the experiences that shaped Ghanaian society during the colonial period.

About this Collection

Everyday life in the colonial Gold Coast was shaped by local traditions, economic activities, family relationships, and changing social conditions. Across towns and villages, people maintained cultural practices while adapting to new opportunities and challenges under colonial rule.

This collection brings together selected historical resources that offer glimpses into ordinary people's experiences, highlighting their work, communities, education, and cultural expressions.
