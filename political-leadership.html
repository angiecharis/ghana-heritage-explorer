J. B. Danquah

1895–1965 · Gold Coast / Ghana

Joseph Boakye Danquah was a Ghanaian lawyer, scholar, and political leader. He was a founding member of the United Gold Coast Convention (UGCC) and played an important role in Ghana's struggle for independence.

Ako Adjei

1916–2002 · Gold Coast / Ghana

Ako Adjei was a Ghanaian lawyer, politician, and independence activist. He was a founding member of the United Gold Coast Convention (UGCC) and one of the Big Six associated with Ghana's struggle for independence. He later served as Ghana's Minister for Foreign Affairs.
