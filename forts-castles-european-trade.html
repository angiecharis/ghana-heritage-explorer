Forts, Castles and European Trade

Explore the historic forts and castles along Ghana's coastline, from Beyin in the west to Keta in the east.

Discover how these sites shaped trade, connections and European powers, the transatlantic slave trade, colonial administration, and Ghana's coastal history.

About this Collection

Ghana's coastline is home to numerous historic forts and castles built and occupied by different European powers over several centuries. These sites served various purposes, including trade, military defence, administration, and the imprisonment of enslaved Africans before their forced transportation across the Atlantic.

This collection introduces selected forts and castles, their individual histories, and their significance in Ghana's cultural heritage.
