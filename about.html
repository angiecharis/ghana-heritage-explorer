About Ghana Heritage Explorer

Discovering Ghana's past, connecting people to its history.

Ghana Heritage Explorer is a curated digital heritage platform that brings together links to historical photographs, documents, newspapers, books,
maps, and audiovisual collections preserved by trusted archives, libraries, museums, and heritage institutions.

Our Aim

To make Ghana's rich history and cultural heritage easier to discover, explore, and appreciate.

Our Mission

To make Ghana's historical and cultural heritage more accessible by connecting people to reliable digital resources preserved by archives, libraries, museums, and other heritage institutions.

Through carefully curated collections, Ghana Heritage Explorer seeks to encourage historical discovery, support learning, and inspire greater appreciation of Ghana's diverse heritage.

Our Goals

  • Improve access: Help people discover Ghanaian historical resources available through trusted digital collections.
  • Support learning: Provide useful starting points for students, educators, researchers, and the general public.
  • Celebrate Ghana's heritage: Highlight the country's history, traditions, cultural diversity, and historical experiences.
  • Encourage exploration: Connect visitors directly to original sources so they can discover and interpret historical materials for themselves.

How Ghana Heritage Explorer Works

Ghana Heritage Explorer is a curated gateway to historical resources, not a repository that stores or owns the original collections.

Visitors can browse historical themes or resource categories, read brief descriptions, and follow links to materials made available by archives, libraries, museums, and other heritage institutions.

Access conditions may vary depending on the institution. Some resources can be viewed or downloaded freely, while others may require registration or online borrowing.
