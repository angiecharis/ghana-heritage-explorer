Railways, Roads and Colonial Development

Explore the development of railways, roads, ports, and other infrastructure in the Gold Coast during the colonial period.

Discover how these projects connected towns and communities, supported the movement of people and goods, and transformed trade and transportation. Through historical photographs, maps, and documents, learn about the people, places, and decisions that shaped Ghana's transport infrastructure.

About this Collection

During the colonial period, the Gold Coast experienced significant developments in transportation and infrastructure, including the construction of railways, roads, bridges, and harbours. These projects connected important trading centres, mining areas, agricultural communities, and coastal ports.

While much of this infrastructure was developed to support colonial administration and the extraction and export of resources, it also influenced settlement patterns, economic activities, and the movement of people. This collection explores these developments and their lasting impact on Ghana.
