The Road to Independence

Explore the Gold Coast's journey towards independence through political movements, protests, elections and individuals who challenged British colonial rule. Discover the events that shaped the struggle for self-government and led to Ghana's independence on 6 March 1957.

About this Collection

The Gold Coast's journey towards independence was shaped by growing demands for political representation, economic justice and self-government. From the 1948 Accra riots to the emergence of nationalist political movements, ordinary citizens, ex-servicemen, workers and political leaders played important roles in challenging British colonial rule.

This collection brings together historical documents, photographs, articles and other trusted resources that trace the major events and political developments leading to Ghana's independence on 6 March 1957. It offers visitors an opportunity to explore the people, movements and decisions that shaped the birth of an independent nation.

The 1948 Accra Riots

Explore the events of 28 February 1948, when a peaceful march by Gold Coast ex-servicemen ended in a deadly confrontation with colonial police. The shootings sparked widespread protests and unrest, marking a turning point in the Gold Coast's struggle for self-government.

The Formation of the Convention People's Party (1949)

Explore the founding of the Convention People's Party (CPP) by Kwame Nkrumah on 12 June 1949, following disagreements within the United Gold Coast Convention (UGCC). Discover how youth mobilisation, political organisation and growing demands for self-government contributed to the emergence of a new political movement in the Gold Coast.

The Positive Action Campaign of 1950

Explore how Kwame Nkrumah and the Convention People's Party launched the Positive Action Campaign in January 1950, using strikes, boycotts and nonviolent resistance to demand self-government. Discover how the campaign challenged British colonial authority, led to the imprisonment of nationalist leaders and helped shape the Gold Coast's journey towards independence.

The 1951 Gold Coast Elections

Explore the significance of the 1951 Gold Coast elections, a major step towards self-government. Discover how the Convention People's Party (CPP) won a decisive victory while Kwame Nkrumah was imprisoned, leading to his release and appointment as Leader of Government Business. The elections marked an important stage in the Gold Coast's transition towards independence.
